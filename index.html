FAQ

Straight answers.

Do you guarantee a number of leads?

No. Results depend on season, area and budget. You get monthly reporting on spend, leads and what kind of jobs they were, and we adjust from there.

Who owns the leads?

You do. Every lead and customer is yours. Campaigns run under our manager accounts, you get reporting access, and ad spend is billed by Google and Meta to your card, never marked up.

What if my utility doesn't allow meter-mounted devices?

We check before you sign. If GenerLink isn't an option in your area, the campaign can focus on standby generators and surge protection instead, or we'll tell you it isn't a fit.

What does "one installer per territory" mean?

While you're active, we won't run generator campaigns for another electrician in your area. Territories are set by postal code or town list in your agreement.

How long until ads are live?

About two to three weeks from sign-up: landing page and creative in week one, your review in week two, launch in week three.
